# Material archive naming: MAR as a future option

- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Open question

The first BoK Material Package operation clarified that Material represents source/working material rather than Knowledge.

Because of that semantics, a future archive/package acronym **MAR** may be more natural than applying a Knowledge-oriented KAR name to Material.

No naming change is made now. Material Package remains the v1 normative term, and existing KAR terminology around PreparedMaterial export/transport is unchanged.

When Material archive/export requirements become concrete, revisit:

- what KAR formally expands to and transports;
- whether Material needs a distinct archive/export format;
- whether MAR should mean Material Archive / Material Artifact (exact expansion also remains open);
- whether Material Package storage and Material transport should use the same or different formats.

The simplemodeling.org Knowledge Lake is the first operational case to use when making this decision.
